# Night

*<span id="tempo-leitura"></span>. 2/11/26.*
 
---

By day, the reason; by night, passion.
My tears for what i never lived,
To the pains and pleasures stollen, I dedicate.

Cowardly steps towards my false beloved.
Beloved! Thou art the poison that warms my veins.
I imagine our transubstantiation in the kiss.
xenomorph! Thou canst not be merely a woman,

To the lovers, say: be fortunate and rejoice.
Others, be content with you misery!

Between letters and waters, I dedicate,
this poem to thee.
