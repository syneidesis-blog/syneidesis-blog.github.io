# Good Night, Dear Werther!

*<span id="tempo-leitura"></span>. 11/8/26.*

---

In the cold silence of the night, my mind finds peace,
My body rests upon a lightweight cradle,
It stirs my senses in a sudden nocturnal outburst.
Oh, sweet cerulean vision, how warmly you fill me!
I tear it from the realm of imagery with my stormy force.

Thou tearest me from my poor reality;
Thou art theophanic, thy perfection is ontological:
The insertion of All Nature in one woman!
Blessed! Perfection! Sacred! Serene! Saint!
thou art my entire reality!

Kiss me! Love me! Take me, Lotte!
Thy moist lips are as natural honey;
Thy blessed purity purifies all my sin.
All my suppressed desire increases at thy presence;
Our kiss has a divine sonority;
The heavens and this world reconcile before it.
I cover thy enchanting form with a thousand kisses.
Beautiful youth! Thou art the world-soul!

Suddenly, at dawn, thou leavest me,
Alone, I dwell in reason's underground.
My senses suffer from thy absence!
Damned! Fallen! Profane! Conflicted! Sinner!
Wretched being! What a fool you are!
How couldst thou believe she would stand by thy side?
I remain alone, just with my sorrowful tears.
