# The last day of Werther

*<span id="tempo-leitura"></span>. 11/13/26.*

---

Wretched being! What a fool you are!
It is over! She shall never see me again!
In the penumbra of solitude, her hand takes shape,
Her honey-sweet and beautiful hand, soft as a lamb's wool.
She was never mine; Albert has taken her from me!
She was mine, meticulous deceiver!

What subtle move! Give me the weapons,
Which shall expand my limitations to infinity,
At least, so that I may join her.
My fragments shall spread through the air,
My primordial desires shall be united with thy form.
Albert, I thank thee for the solution!

In my freezing hands, i grasp the fervent metal,
I load thy bullets, as a physician who heals,
Antemortem, with my final sighs, I guide the smooth barrel of the gun towards my soul.
Oh, what a sweet moment! The expansion of the cosmos!
Farewell, Charlotte, we shall meet again soon!
From absolute nothingness, towards infinity transcedental.
