# Brasil, Love Me ou Leave Me

*<span id="tempo-leitura"></span>. 9/11/26.*

---

The clamour of the pavilion proclaims my devotion;
My veins spill blood for thee.
Your strong hands silence my desire.
My Brazil, I love you! I love you!
No one can hold back our youth!

Your scattered flowers enchant me
My folk were scattered by you,
There, la, la, la, la
Stay of love, let us love thee and abide with thee!
Thy beautiful nature, idolised, encloses us.
Great maiden, rule us with thy scarlet cloak!
My Brazil, I love you! I love you!
To all exiles of thy love, lamentations!

In thy bracing waters, bathe me,
Oh, what a suffocating lovely love!
It warms my spirit with its ember,
Oh, what a hot lovely love!
I sit beside thee, thy ferrous presence,
Oh, what a metallic lovely love!
My Brazil, I love you! I love you!
Thy passionate ardour annuls me!

Miracle! Years of passion!
Thy medic patron calms me,
Thy forces immobilize me,
My patriotism drives me to madness!
My Brazil, I love you! I love you!
Thy nights are more beautiful!
