# The Pleasuares Of Young Werther

*<span id="tempo-leitura"></span>. 11/13/26.*

---
Oh Wilhelm! She - she has finally kissed me, she! Canst thou believe it?
Thy harmonious voice said: will stay with me, WI-TH ME!!!
Wilhelm, it was angelic, I am deeply enamoured, my dear friend!
Oh, dear friend, how can one reduce a kiss to mere letters?

I confess my fear of desecrating the passion, this world-spirit!
Oh my friend, imagine thy nerves resting upon the most beautiful cloud!
It seemed eternal; All reality had prepared itself for this beautiful moment!
The world rejoiced and was reconciled, Nature at peace!

My brother, at the moment, my poor heart does nothing but weep!
Weeping for her absence, should something so venerable not be eternal?!
Love, Beauty, the Sacred, they are the very particles of this world!
Wilhelm, i speak so much, yet reveal nothing. Forgive me!
Any attempt to describe this would be a lie! A farce!

My dear, i shall illustrate it thus: She has blinded my senses with her lips!
All pass, present, or future desire, fades before their image!
How can one desire, how can one love, how can one fell anything after beholding such beauty?!
The keys to my life were her crimson lips! Wilhelm, Wilhelm!
My friend, i wish thee to experience the same overwhelming sensation one day, so transformative!
Oh Wilhelm, how can a person concentrate embody all perfection??!!
She must be sacred, made especially by God for me!!!
Wilhelm, I believe I am lost in love!
